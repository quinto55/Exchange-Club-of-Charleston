Privacy

This demo keeps almost nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere.

What this demo collects

Nothing. The site sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Every form in this demo — giving, RSVP, the membership application and contact — runs only in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

What it keeps on your device

  • ecc.portal in session storage remembers that you opened the member-portal preview. It clears when you close the tab.
  • ecc.portal.state in local storage keeps your demo shift and committee choices. “Reset demo” in the portal clears it.

Photos from the club’s albums

Gallery and story photos load from the club’s photo storage at clubrunner.blob.core.windows.net. Like any image host, it receives your IP address and browser details when it serves them.

At launch

This page describes the demo only. A live site would replace it with the club’s own privacy policy.
